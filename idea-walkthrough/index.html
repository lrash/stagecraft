Stagecraft runs on free API tiers. You need at least one. OpenRouter alone is enough — one key covers dozens of free models across several vendors.

Where your key goes: into this browser on this computer, and nowhere else. Requests go from your browser straight to the provider. There is no Stagecraft server, no account, and no telemetry — nothing to breach. Use Forget everything below to wipe it.

Where your idea goes: your key stays here, but the text you write does not — each stage is sent to the model vendor the router picks, and free tiers commonly log and train on what they receive. Stagecraft names that vendor next to the Run button before you send, never only after. Do not put anything confidential into a free-tier run.

Keys are free and take about a minute each. No card is asked for by any of these. Daily allowances reset on their own — Stagecraft counts what you've used and tells you when a tier is spent.
